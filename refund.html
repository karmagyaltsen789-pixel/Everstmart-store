Return & Refund Policy

At Everstmart, we aim to provide quality products and a satisfactory shopping experience. Please read our return and refund policy carefully before placing an order.

1. Returns

We accept returns only in cases where the product received is damaged, defective, incorrect, or significantly different from the product ordered.

Customers should contact us within 48 hours of receiving the order if there is a problem with the product.

2. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or you receive the wrong product, please contact us through WhatsApp or email as soon as possible.

To help us process your request, please provide:

  • Order ID
  • Photos of the product received
  • Photos of the packaging, if applicable
  • A brief description of the issue

3. Non-Returnable Products

Certain products may not be eligible for return due to their nature, hygiene considerations, or because they are consumable.

  • Incense and other consumable products
  • Products that have been used or damaged after delivery
  • Products without the original packaging, where applicable
  • Products damaged due to improper handling by the customer

4. Refunds

If a return or refund is approved, the refund amount will be processed after the returned product has been received and inspected, where applicable.

Refunds may be issued through the original payment method or another mutually agreed method.

5. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are generally processed within 5–7 business days after approval. The actual time for the amount to appear in your account may depend on your bank or payment provider.

6. Exchange

Product exchanges may be considered for damaged, defective, or incorrectly supplied products, subject to product availability.

7. Cancellation

Order cancellation requests should be made as soon as possible. Once an order has been processed or shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible.

8. Return Shipping

If the return is due to a damaged, defective, or incorrect product, Everstmart may arrange or reimburse reasonable return shipping costs, subject to verification.
