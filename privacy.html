Privacy Policy

At Everstmart, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website and purchasing our products.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order through Everstmart, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address
  • PIN code
  • Product and order information

We only collect information that is reasonably necessary to process and fulfil your order.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected may be used to:

  • Process and confirm your orders
  • Arrange delivery of your products
  • Contact you regarding your order
  • Provide order updates through WhatsApp
  • Respond to customer enquiries
  • Improve our products and services

3. WhatsApp Orders

Everstmart may use WhatsApp to receive and communicate regarding customer orders. When you choose to place an order through WhatsApp, the information you provide may be shared through WhatsApp in order to process your order.

4. Payments

Everstmart may provide UPI payment options for purchases. Payment transactions are processed through the applicable payment application or banking service.

Everstmart does not intentionally collect or store your UPI PIN, bank password, debit card PIN, or other confidential banking credentials.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Your information may be shared when reasonably necessary to fulfil your order, such as with delivery or shipping service providers.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information provided to us. However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Cookies and Website Data

Everstmart may use basic website technologies such as browser storage to remember shopping cart information and improve the website experience.

Our current shopping cart may use your browser's local storage to temporarily store products added to your cart.

8. Children's Privacy

Everstmart does not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of making purchases.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our website, services, or business practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page.
